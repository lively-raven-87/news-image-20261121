A black-and-white newspaper front page summing up 21 November 2026 world news in a single 1024×1024 image. The lead story is the capture of FBI most-wanted cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores ("El Chapo Isidro") in Sinaloa, Mexico, followed by the AI stock sell-off dragging global markets into correction territory, Russian missiles hitting Kryvyi Rih and landing in Poland, Trump weighing a new strike on Iran, and the COP31 climate summit blowing past its deadline — with a bottom ticker strip covering the Peru quake, Denmark's expulsion of the Russian envoy, Anthropic's $81.3bn IPO, and the ASEAN summit opening in Manila. The image is generated from scratch by `draw.py` (Python + Pillow) in a classic newspaper style.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and validates it: a valid PNG, at most 1 MiB, at most 1024×1024 pixels. To regenerate the image itself, run `python3 draw.py`.
